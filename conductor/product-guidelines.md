# Product Guidelines: AYSO Region 154 Volunteer Dashboard

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- **Visual Theme:** Modern athletic dashboard with vibrant sports styling, high contrast, and responsive layout.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary: AYSO Royal Blue & Navy (`#003366`, `#0f172a`)
  - Accent: Vibrant Soccer Gold / Yellow (`#f59e0b`, `#fbbf24`) for playoff qualification highlights
  - Status Indicators: Emerald Green (`#10b981`) for completed/qualified goals, Crimson (`#ef4444`) for conflicts/unfulfilled requirements, Amber (`#f59e0b`) for pending items
  - Surfaces: Clean slate dark and light card surfaces with crisp typography and subtle borders
- **Typography:** Modern, readable system fonts (Inter, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto) optimized for rapid glanceability under bright sunlight on Saturday game days.

## Voice, Tone & Messaging
- **Tone:** Welcoming, transparent, encouraging, and sportsmanship-centric.
- **Community Focus:** Celebrates volunteer contributions across coaching, refereeing, and field operations.
- **Clarity on Rules:** Unambiguous language regarding playoff qualification (e.g., "17 Points to Playoffs", "Referee Cap: 10 pts max").
- **Error & Warning Messaging:** Constructive and actionable (e.g., "Conflict detected on Field 1 at 9:00 AM — please review against game card").

## UX Principles
- **Mobile-First Game Day Performance:** Lightning-fast rendering on mobile cellular connections with minimal asset payload and aggressive client-side caching.
- **Zero-Friction Access:** Public standings require zero logins, passwords, or complex navigation.
- **Privacy by Default:** Contact information (emails, phone numbers) is never rendered on the public dashboard.
- **Instant Visual Feedback:** Clear status badges, progress bars toward the 17-point target, and intuitive sync indicators.
- **Defensive Data Handling:** Resilient against network drops, sheet formatting anomalies, and duplicate form submissions.
