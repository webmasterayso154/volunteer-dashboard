Standard operating routine for weekly volunteer tracking

Monday Morning

Referee Admin or Delegate

Field Director / Webmaster

Volunteer Duty Season Cap Rules & Notes
Playoff Requirement 17 pts Total points needed for tournament eligibility.
Referee Assignments 10 pts On-field referee duty (Dual AR awarded 1 pt each; NOCRA centers earn 0).
Friday Night Setup 5 pts 1 point per hour shift.
Field Marshal Shifts 2 pts Can be completed on same Saturday or separate dates.
Picture Day & Picnic 2 pts Volunteer event assistance.
Certified Referee Bonus 5 pts Administrative bonus for certified team referees entered in Team_Awards.
MatchTrak Compliance 2 pts Awarded for complete MatchTrak rosters by September 28.
