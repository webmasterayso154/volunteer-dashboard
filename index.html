Playoff qualification requirement

For U8 - U19 & EXTRA: Teams must earn a minimum of 17 total verified points to be eligible for post-season play (Cypress Cup and/or Playoffs)[cite: 1]. Playground, U5 & U6 are non-competitive divisions[cite: 1].

Category caps (26 points possible)[cite: 1]

• Referee Assignments: Up to 10 points for completed on-field games (up to 17 total including pre-season certified refs and early MatchTrak sign-ups)[cite: 1].
• Field Marshal: Capped at 2 points (2 shifts per team)[cite: 1].
• Friday Night Setup: Capped at 5 points (1 point per Friday night attendance)[cite: 1].
• Picture Day: Capped at 2 points (1 point per 1-hour shift)[cite: 1].

Something missing or incorrect?

Duplicate check-ins, time conflicts (same referee at two fields simultaneously), or check-ins exceeding category limits are automatically flagged in red. If an assignment was misallocated, have your coach submit a review request to Cypress154RRA@gmail.com[cite: 1].
