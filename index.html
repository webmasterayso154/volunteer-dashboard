ⓘ Volunteer Credit & Verification Guidelines
  • Verification & Corrections: We are all volunteers and mistakes happen! If an assignment or check-in is missing, duplicate, or appears incorrect, please notify your Head Coach or Team Manager so they can submit a verification request.
  • Board Authority: The AYSO Region 154 Board of Directors retains full authority to review, correct, or manually adjust team volunteer points at any time for administrative accuracy or extenuating circumstances.
  • Playoff Requirement: Teams must earn 17 total verified points during the regular season to qualify for postseason play.
  • Category Maximums: Referee assignments earn 1 pt each (up to 17 pts). Field Marshal duties are capped at 2 pts. Friday Night Setup is capped at 5 pts. Picture Day & Silent Weekend are capped at 2 pts.
