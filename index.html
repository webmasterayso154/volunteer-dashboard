Something missing or wrong?

We are all volunteers and mistakes happen. If an assignment or check-in is missing, duplicated, or looks incorrect, tell your head coach or team manager so they can submit a verification request.

Playoff requirement

Teams must earn 17 total verified points during the regular season to qualify for postseason play.

Category maximums

Referee assignments earn 1 pt each, up to 17. Field Marshal is capped at 2 pts. Friday Night Setup is capped at 5 pts. Picture Day & Silent Weekend are capped at 2 pts.

Board authority

The AYSO Region 154 Board of Directors retains full authority to review, correct, or manually adjust team volunteer points at any time for administrative accuracy or extenuating circumstances.
