Competitive Divisions (U8 - U19 & EXTRA)

Teams must earn a minimum of 17 total verified points to qualify for post-season play (Cypress Cup and Playoffs). Points are earned through Referee assignments (up to 10 on-field), Field Marshal (max 2), Friday Setup (max 5), and Picture Day (max 2).

U5 & U6 Divisions

Target is 8 volunteer points out of 9 possible. Points are earned through Saturday morning setup/takedown (up to 5 points), Field Marshal shifts during Weeks 4 & 10 (max 2 points), and Picture Picnic Day (max 2 points).

Playground Division

Target is 4 volunteer points (4 points possible). Points are earned through Field Marshal shifts during Weeks 4 & 10 (max 2 points) and Picture Picnic Day shifts (max 2 points).
