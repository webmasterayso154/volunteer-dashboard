🙏 A Heartfelt Thank You to Our Region 154 Volunteers

AYSO Region 154 exists entirely because of the generosity, time, and heart of our families. To every coach, referee, field marshal, setup & takedown volunteer, team manager, and parent who stepped up in the early mornings and warm afternoons—thank you. Your dedication and sportsmanship gave our young athletes a safe, joyful, and unforgettable Fall 2026 soccer season!

Competitive Divisions (10U through 19U)

Cypress Cup Qualification: Competitive divisions (10U through 19U) must earn a minimum of 17 verified points to qualify for post-season play in the Cypress Cup. Points are earned through Referee assignments (up to 10 on-field), Certified Ref Bonus (max 5), Early Ref Scheduling Incentive (max 2), Field Marshal (2 baseline shifts; up to 4 points max with Cypress Cup boost), Friday Setup (max 5), and Picture Day (max 2).

08U (8U) Division

Cypress Cup Qualification: Teams in 8U must earn a minimum of 15 verified points to qualify for the Cypress Cup. Points are earned through Referee assignments, Certified Ref Bonus, MatchTrak Bonus, Saturday Field Setup, and Field Marshal shifts (2 baseline shifts; up to 4 points max).

05U & 06U (5U & 6U) Divisions

Division Goal: Teams in 5U & 6U have a season target of 7 verified points. Points are earned through Saturday morning setup/takedown (up to 5 points), Field Marshal shifts (up to 4 points max), and Picture Picnic Day (max 2 points).

Playground / Schoolyard Division

Target is 4 verified points. Points are earned through Field Marshal shifts (up to 4 points) and Picture Picnic Day shifts (max 2 points).
