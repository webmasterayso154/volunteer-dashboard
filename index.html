ⓘ Playoff Qualification & Point Policy Guidelines
  • Playoff Requirement: Teams must earn 17 total volunteer points during the regular season to qualify for postseason play.
  • Referee Assignments: Up to 17 points may be earned via Center or Assistant Referee assignments. (Club linespersons earn 0 points).
  • Category Maximums: Field Marshal duties are capped at 2 points. Friday Night Setup duties are capped at 5 points.
  • Verification: Points display as Pending until volunteer Live Scan and Safe Haven compliance checks are fully reconciled by tech ops.
